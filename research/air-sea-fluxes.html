Research

Machine learning for air‑sea fluxes

Addressing oceanographic data scarcity in how climate models represent air-sea fluxes.

Paper drafting is in progress. Results, the preprint, the code repository, and an interactive results explorer will be published here with the posted preprint.

Overview

Observations of air-sea fluxes are scarce, which limits how well climate models represent the exchange of heat and momentum between ocean and atmosphere.

This project develops a hybrid physics–machine learning workflow that:

  • trains ensembles of neural networks on 3 million synthetic samples,
  • fine-tunes them via transfer learning to ~10,000 in-situ observations, and
  • employs causal discovery methods (PCMCI+) to illuminate directional relationships between meteorological drivers and air-sea fluxes, enabling physically grounded interpretation of machine learning predictions.

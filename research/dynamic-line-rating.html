Research

Wind energy & dynamic line rating

A risk-mitigation framework for wind energy along transmission corridors.

Paper drafting is in progress. Results, the preprint, the code repository, and an interactive results explorer will be published here with the posted preprint.

Overview

The same wind that drives generation also cools transmission lines and raises their dynamic line rating (DLR), so transfer capacity peaks just as wind output does.

This project applies spatial analysis to develop a risk-mitigation framework for wind energy along transmission corridors. It:

  • predicts wind speed between sparse measurement points,
  • forecasts with calibrated confidence intervals, and
  • culminates in sensor-placement recommendations to improve safety and reduce curtailment.
